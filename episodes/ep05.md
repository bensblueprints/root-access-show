# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 5 — "the_previous_holder"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — DAY]

The fifth name hangs over everything. Zero-Day has pinned it to the central screen — a ghost's name, erased from the ledger but still signing files. The crew is hunting it like an animal.

ZERO-DAY: The previous holder's identity was wiped from every record the corporation keeps. Every one. That takes an order, not an accident.

SWARM TECH: Who erases a dead man?

GHOST WALKER: Someone who needs him to stay dead.

NEURAL BURN: The static says he's not dead. It says he's *before* us. It says he's us.

ZERO-DAY: There's one sealed file left. A pre-conversion contract archive, under corporate legal seal. A lawyer held the originals. If anyone kept a copy the corporation couldn't scrub, it's him.

SWARM TECH: A corporate lawyer's going to hand us sealed files because we ask nicely?

ZERO-DAY: He's dying, Swarm. The conversion swallowed him — debts he never agreed to, re-denominated into credits he can't pay. The corporation he served wrote him off. He's got nothing left to lose but the file.

GHOST WALKER: That's how the machine makes its own leaks.

ZERO-DAY: That's how we cut it open.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The fifth name on the central screen, the crew's shadows long across the basement.]

---

[SCENE 2 — INT. ABANDONED LAW OFFICE — THE SPRAWL — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → abandoned law office, the Sprawl]

A flooded office above the waterline, shelves of paper molder in the damp, and a single lantern burns on a desk. The LEDGER LAWYER sits behind it — gaunt, shaking, his fine coat gone to ruin. Zero-Day and Ghost Walker enter through the back.

LEDGER LAWYER: You're the ones they're looking for. You shouldn't be here.

ZERO-DAY: Neither should you. You worked for the corporation that killed you.

LEDGER LAWYER: I drafted their contracts. I made their erasures legal. And when they re-denominated my mother's debt, they called it "acceptable loss" to my face.

GHOST WALKER: Then help us. The sealed file.

LEDGER LAWYER: The previous holder. You want to know who held the Key-Staff before you.

ZERO-DAY: You know about the Key-Staff.

LEDGER LAWYER: I *wrote* the custody paperwork for it. I know it moves from hand to hand like a curse. And I know the corporation spent thirty years making sure no one remembers the hand before yours.

He slides a data-slate across the desk. It's cracked, old, still sealed with a corporate wax that's gone brittle.

LEDGER LAWYER: There's only one record left. They scrubbed the rest. And that's how you'll know you're right — because of how hard they tried to make it not exist.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The dying lawyer's hands on the cracked data-slate, lantern-light on the flooded office.]

---

[SCENE 3 — INT. ABANDONED LAW OFFICE — CONTINUOUS]

The slate unseals. Zero-Day reads it, and the color drains from the room.

ZERO-DAY: His name.

LEDGER LAWYER: Say it. It hasn't been said out loud in years.

ZERO-DAY: It's been erased from the ledger, the registry, the monument rolls, even the death records. But here — the original custody transfer. He existed.

GHOST WALKER: What's his name?

ZERO-DAY: (slowly) It doesn't match any name I know. But the custody clause lists him as *Bearer of the Key-Staff, First Cycle*.

LEDGER LAWYER: "First Cycle." You see it now, don't you? The corporation doesn't think of the city as a place with a history. It thinks of it as a *cycle*. And your predecessor was the bearer of the first one.

GHOST WALKER: First cycle. Implying there were others.

ZERO-DAY: Implying there's a *current* one. And the corporation's been resetting it.

The lawyer coughs — a wet, terrible sound — and grips the desk.

LEDGER LAWYER: They don't just erase people. They erase *versions* of the city. And they keep one file so the next cycle has a template. You're looking at the template's old owner.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day holding the slate, the lawyer's gaunt face in the lantern light.]

---

[SCENE 4 — INT. ARCOLOGY — KAIROS'S COMMAND — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: the Sprawl → the Arcology]

The wall of screens shows the Sprawl tightening — sweeps, checkpoints, a noose of drones. NEXUS COMMANDER stands at attention, command baton raised, two spotter drones whispering at his shoulder.

KAIROS: They found the lawyer.

NEXUS COMMANDER: I have his office pinned, Director. Give me the order and I'll take all four of them tonight.

KAIROS: No. You'll take the file first. Then them.

NEXUS COMMANDER: The file is a corpse's name. What does it matter if the rebels read it?

KAIROS: (turning, slowly) Because the name is a door, Commander. And behind that door is a version of the city that *must not be remembered*. If the NU11 open it, the harvest is ruined. Do you understand what "ruined" costs?

NEXUS COMMANDER: Then I'll burn the file and the lawyer with it.

KAIROS: You'll do exactly as you're told. Find them. But leave Zero-Day alive. He is not a rebel to be killed. He is an asset to be *replayed*.

Nexus Commander's certainty falters, just for a frame.

NEXUS COMMANDER: ...Replayed.

KAIROS: Same as last time, Commander. Now go.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Kairos's helm and the wall of screens, Nexus Commander's rigid silhouette.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. ABANDONED LAW OFFICE — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

The lawyer is fading fast. He pushes a final fragment across the desk — a photograph, paper, water-stained at the edges.

LEDGER LAWYER: Last file. The custody transfer's attached portrait. The only image of him that survived the purge.

ZERO-DAY: Why show me this?

LEDGER LAWYER: Because you need to see it before they come for it. Look.

Zero-Day lifts the photograph into the lantern light. Ghost Walker leans in. And both of them go still.

The man in the photograph is holding the Key-Staff. His posture, his hands, his coat — all of it is wrong in a way that makes the room shrink. Because the man in the photograph has a *hologram head*. A skull of light and running code, flickering in a frame that's decades old.

GHOST WALKER: Zero. That's you.

ZERO-DAY: (a whisper) No. That's him. That's the previous holder. And he has my head.

LEDGER LAWYER: (smiling, faintly) I told you. You're not hunting a dead man. You're hunting the shape of the thing you already are.

The lawyer's breath rattles. Somewhere below, a checkpoint siren begins to rise.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The water-stained photograph in Zero-Day's shaking hand — a hologram head that is his own.]

---

[CLIFFHANGER: The lawyer's last file is a photo of the previous holder — and the man in the photo has a hologram head, exactly like Zero-Day's.]
