# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 4 — "pre-registered"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — DAY]

The mood is worse. The grid has fully hardened; below, the Sprawl moves under a ceiling of drones. Around the table, the four argue over a holo-schematic of a Root Terminal — a hardened node sunk into the flooded under-levels.

SWARM TECH: A Root Terminal? Those are the corporation's nerve endings. You don't *break into* one. You get disassembled near one.

ZERO-DAY: We need the conversion ledger. The real one. Who got a credit line, who got flagged, and *when*. If the system knew my code before I wrote it, the ledger will say how.

GHOST WALKER: And if it knew our names too?

ZERO-DAY: Then we'll know we're not hunting a leak. We're hunting a script.

NEURAL BURN: The static says we should look. It says the answer's already written down, waiting for us.

SWARM TECH: The static's not a source, Neural.

NEURAL BURN: It's more reliable than anyone in this room right now.

The line lands. Nobody answers it.

ZERO-DAY: I'll pull the fragment. Ghost walks point. Swarm covers our exit with drones. Neural runs the decrypt from here.

GHOST WALKER: If the grid predicts us, I want a dead drop, not a rescue.

ZERO-DAY: Fine. Dead drop.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The holo-schematic of the Root Terminal, the four gathered tight around it.]

---

[SCENE 2 — INT. ROOT TERMINAL — UNDER-LEVEL — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → Root Terminal, flooded under-level]

The terminal breathes in the dark — a tower of cold light sunk into black water, cables dripping like roots into the flood. Zero-Day wades in, Key-Staff ready. Ghost Walker moves ahead, poncho eating the reflections, neural spike glinting.

GHOST WALKER: Clear to the core. No drones. No lenses.

ZERO-DAY: That's not reassuring. A terminal this deep should be guarded.

GHOST WALKER: Maybe it isn't, because they don't need to.

Zero-Day plugs the Key-Staff into the terminal's data spine. The tower flares, and for a second the whole under-level lights up like a drowned cathedral.

ZERO-DAY: I'm in. Pulling the ledger now.

The data pours — millions of names, credit lines, biometric hashes, timestamps. Zero-Day's fingers fly, filtering, searching.

ZERO-DAY: Filtering for pre-broadcast entries... there. A fragment. It's small. It's—

He stops.

ZERO-DAY: Ghost. Come here.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day at the terminal core, Key-Staff blazing, the flooded under-level lit like a cathedral.]

---

[SCENE 3 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

The fragment is up on every screen. Four names. The NU11's own — not handles, but the names underneath them. Each is marked as an INSURGENT, pre-registered, with a timestamp that precedes the emergency broadcast by weeks.

SWARM TECH: That's... that's us. That's *us*.

GHOST WALKER: Dated before the broadcast. Before the conversion was even announced.

ZERO-DAY: Someone filed these names as insurgents before we ever picked up a gun or a keyboard. Before the grid. Before the credits.

NEURAL BURN: Before we chose.

SWARM TECH: What do you mean, "before we chose"?

NEURAL BURN: Look at the dates. We didn't exist as NU11 when these were filed. Zero-Day didn't have the Key-Staff. I hadn't been burned yet.

GHOST WALKER: Then someone predicted us down to our names.

ZERO-DAY: No. Someone *pre-registered* us. Like booking a room for guests who haven't arrived.

The room goes cold. The four glance at each other, and for the first time, there's distance in it.

SWARM TECH: How does a system know our names before we have names? Somebody in this room—

GHOST WALKER: Careful.

SWARM TECH: —somebody talked to them. Or somebody's been on the inside all along.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The four names glowing on the screens, the crew's faces lit and suspicious.]

---

[SCENE 4 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

The trust cracks in real time. Swarm Tech paces. Ghost Walker stands still, too still. Zero-Day reads and re-reads the fragment. Neural Burn watches them all, the cables at his skull twitching.

SWARM TECH: I want to know how my name got into their system before I ever set foot in it. I want to know who put it there.

GHOST WALKER: We all got put there. All four. Same hand.

SWARM TECH: Then one of us is that hand.

ZERO-DAY: No. Look at the file. The author signature isn't any of us.

SWARM TECH: It could be forged.

ZERO-DAY: It's encrypted with a key that *predates* the Key-Staff. It's older than what I carry. Nobody in this room could write that.

GHOST WALKER: Then who.

ZERO-DAY: That's what I'm decrypting. Give me the room.

The others fall silent, but the silence is heavy — not the easy quiet of old friends, but the watchful quiet of people beginning to count each other's exits.

NEURAL BURN: (low, to no one) It was always going to be four names. There were always going to be four.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The four scattered now across the room, distance between them, the fragment still glowing.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. BASEMENT — SERVER CLOSET — NIGHT]

[ROOM CHANGE: main floor → server closet]

Zero-Day works alone in the closet, the Key-Staff laid across his knees, the fragment's author signature unfolding under his tools. Hours pass in the glow. Then the encryption gives, and a name resolves — not a corporation employee ID, not a department code.

ZERO-DAY: (barely audible) That's not corporate.

He calls the others in. They crowd the door again, and this time the distrust is gone, replaced by something worse.

SWARM TECH: Whose name is that?

ZERO-DAY: It's not an employee. There's no ledger line, no badge. But the encryption key matches the Key-Staff's *previous* signature. The one from before me.

GHOST WALKER: The previous holder.

ZERO-DAY: The person who held the Key-Staff before I did. The one I took it from — or was given it by. I don't remember which.

NEURAL BURN: He's dead.

ZERO-DAY: He's supposed to be. This file says he pre-registered the four of us. Weeks before the conversion. A dead man filled out our paperwork.

SWARM TECH: So either he's not dead—

ZERO-DAY: —or the system filed us under a ghost's name, and the ghost has been running this whole thing from the start.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day's face in the closet glow, the fifth name on screen above the Key-Staff.]

---

[CLIFFHANGER: Zero-Day decrypts the pre-registration's author signature — not a corporate employee, but a fifth name: the person who held the Key-Staff before Zero-Day did, a dead man.]
