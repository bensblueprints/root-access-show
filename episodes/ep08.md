# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 8 — "the_clock"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

The old broadcast has broken them open. The four stand apart now, voices rising, the easy trust of the first episodes gone. The recording loops on a dead screen.

SWARM TECH: We can't just *believe* this. It's a recording. It could be a fake, a plant, a trap the corporation left for us to find.

ZERO-DAY: The timestamp is real. The seal is real. It's older than the flood.

SWARM TECH: Then maybe the flood's a lie too! Maybe everything's a lie and we're just four idiots shouting in a hole!

GHOST WALKER: If the conversion already happened, then our war already happened. So what are we?

NEURAL BURN: We're the echo. We're the part that plays after the real thing's over.

ZERO-DAY: That's enough.

NEURAL BURN: No. It's not. You all keep asking what the static means. It means we've done this before. Every step. Every fight. And we're going to do it again because the machine wants us to.

SWARM TECH: You don't know that.

NEURAL BURN: I'm the only one who does. The only one who can *hear* it. And none of you want to listen because the truth sounds like losing.

The room cracks. Swarm Tech turns away. Ghost Walker goes still. And Zero-Day stands in the middle, unable to hold any of it together.

ZERO-DAY: We don't splinter. Not now.

NEURAL BURN: Too late.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The four pulled apart in the basement, the old broadcast still looping between them.]

---

[SCENE 2 — EXT. THE SPRAWL — SWEEP — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → the Sprawl]

The splinter sends them out into the city at the worst possible moment. A brutal corporate sweep tears through the Sprawl — checkpoints, drones, white-plated soldiers pouring down the flooded streets. Neural Burn, alone and wired and careless, is the one they corner.

ARC SENTINEL: (over the loudspeaker, calm) Citizen. Present your ledger and remain still.

NEURAL BURN: (to the drones, to the soldiers, to himself) I hear you. I hear all of you. You're already dead and you don't know it.

Arc Sentinel braced on stabilizer legs, arc projector charged, advances. The polite word for what it does is "deterrence."

ARC SENTINEL: This is your final instruction.

Neural Burn doesn't run. He turns toward the projector like a man greeting an old friend, and the cables at his skull crackle with the city's static.

NEURAL BURN: It's already happened. I've already been here. So have you.

The projector fires. Neural Burn goes down in a net of light, captured — not killed. Arc Sentinel hauls the seer up by the trailing spinal cables, gentle in a way that's almost worse.

ARC SENTINEL: (low) I'm sorry. It's the job.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Neural Burn limp in Arc Sentinel's grip, the sweep roaring around them.]

---

[SCENE 3 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

The crew returns without him. Three figures now, where there were four. The basement has never felt so empty.

SWARM TECH: They took Neural. They took him because I let him walk out alone.

ZERO-DAY: No. They took him because I let us splinter.

GHOST WALKER: We get him back.

SWARM TECH: How? They'll have him in the Arcology by now. In a cell. Wiped, or worse.

ZERO-DAY: Neural's brain is already burned. Wiping it won't work. That's why they took him *alive*. They want what's in his head. The recordings. The voice.

GHOST WALKER: Then we don't have much time.

SWARM TECH: So what do we do, Zero? You're the one who's supposed to know the next move!

Zero-Day has no answer. For the first time, the master hacker stands in the middle of his own basement and comes up empty.

ZERO-DAY: I don't know. I don't know anymore.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Three figures in the empty basement, the fourth chair conspicuously bare.]

---

[SCENE 4 — INT. BASEMENT — LATER]

A message arrives — not on their rig, but through the grid itself, addressed to Zero-Day by name. A clean white card, Terable Corp's seal, an invitation with no sender but a clock face.

ZERO-DAY: It's from Kairos.

SWARM TECH: Burn it.

ZERO-DAY: He wants a private meeting. Me. Alone. No weapons. Top of the city.

GHOST WALKER: It's a trap.

ZERO-DAY: It's worse than a trap. It's an invitation. He wants to show me something.

SWARM TECH: And you want to go.

ZERO-DAY: I want to know what he knows about the loop. About the old broadcast. About *me*. And he's offering it.

GHOST WALKER: If you go up, you don't come back down the same.

ZERO-DAY: I'm not the same now, Ghost. I haven't been since the broadcast.

SWARM TECH: What about Neural?

ZERO-DAY: If I go, maybe I bring him back. Maybe the truth's the price of his freedom.

The three lock eyes. Every instinct says don't. But Zero-Day is already reaching for his coat — the coat of living code, the staff in his fist.

ZERO-DAY: I'm going up.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day reaching for the Key-Staff, the invitation card glowing on the table.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. ARCOLOGY — KAIROS'S PRIVATE CHAMBER — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → top of the city, Kairos's private chamber]

The highest room in the city. Glass on every side, the flood glittering far below. Zero-Day enters alone, unarmed, the Key-Staff dark at his side. DIRECTOR KAIROS waits with his back to him, clock-faced helm catching the city's light.

KAIROS: You came.

ZERO-DAY: You took my friend.

KAIROS: I took the one who could hear me. I needed a private word with you, and he was the only one who might have warned you what I am before I could say it myself.

ZERO-DAY: What are you?

KAIROS: Patient. That's all. The rest you're about to learn.

Kairos turns. Slowly, deliberately, he reaches up and lifts the clock-faced helm from his head, setting it aside. What's beneath is ordinary, and ancient, and terribly familiar. And when he speaks again, it is not the voice of a corporate director.

KAIROS: You're not the first.

The voice — it stops Zero-Day cold. Because he has never met Kairos before, and yet he *knows* this voice. He has heard it. In the static. Through Neural Burn's jacks. Whispering his name.

KAIROS: We've had this conversation before.

ZERO-DAY: (a whisper) It's you. The voice in the static. The one Neural Burn hears.

KAIROS: I've been calling you for a very long time, Zero-Day. And tonight, you finally picked up.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Kairos's helm set aside, his face lit by the city — and Zero-Day, recognizing the voice at last.]

---

[CLIFFHANGER: Kairos removes his clock-faced helm and says, "You're not the first — we've had this conversation before," and Zero-Day recognizes the voice as the one Neural Burn has been hearing in the static.]
