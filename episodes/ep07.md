# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 7 — "the_old_broadcast"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

Neural Burn has not slept. His halo of holo-screens is now a ring of flickering, half-tuned images, and he sits in the center, jacks sparking, listening. The others hang back, wary.

ZERO-DAY: You've been at this for two days.

NEURAL BURN: I'm close. The static isn't static. It never was. It's got *structure*. Beginnings and ends. A signal, not a sickness.

SWARM TECH: Neural, you've said that before. It's how you got burned in the first place.

NEURAL BURN: No. I got burned because I listened too *loosely*. But now I know what I'm hearing. It's not the city talking. It's the city *replaying*.

GHOST WALKER: Replaying what?

NEURAL BURN: (turning) Everything.

He throws a switch, and the static on his screens begins to *resolve*. Beneath the noise, beneath the hum of the living grid, something else surfaces — a deeper layer, an older signal, like a second city running underneath the first.

NEURAL BURN: Listen.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Neural Burn in the ring of screens, the static beginning to resolve into shapes.]

---

[SCENE 2 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

The room fills with ghost-signals. Faint transmissions, old weather reports, a jingle for a store that no longer exists. The crew leans in, stunned.

SWARM TECH: That's... that's a broadcast from before. Before the flood.

ZERO-DAY: Not before the flood. *Under* it. The Root has layers. We've been scraping the top one. But there's something below — an old city, still powered, still *transmitting*.

NEURAL BURN: It never shut down. They didn't turn off the old city. They just buried it and built a new one on top. And the old one is still down there, still broadcasting, bleeding through the cracks.

GHOST WALKER: Like water through a seawall.

NEURAL BURN: Exactly. And my head — my burned head — is the only antenna in this room that can pick it up.

Zero-Day steps closer to the screens, watching an old traffic camera feed resolve from the noise — a street that no longer exists, a corner that is now underwater.

ZERO-DAY: The flooded city is sitting on top of an older version of itself. Still running. Still remembering.

SWARM TECH: Remembering. That's what you said about the grid. That it remembers.

NEURAL BURN: Because it does. The whole thing is one machine, and the machine never forgets a cycle.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The old traffic camera feed on Neural Burn's screens — a drowned corner, alive again.]

---

[SCENE 3 — INT. ROOT DEEP LAYER — VIA TERMINAL — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → deep Root layer (accessed via terminal)]

Zero-Day and Neural Burn jack into the terminal, descending past the living grid into the layers beneath. The visual is vertigo — code that isn't code but *geography*, an old skyline rendered in dead data, still glowing faintly.

ZERO-DAY: It's a whole city. A whole *version* of the city, preserved down here.

NEURAL BURN: I told you. The old one never died.

ZERO-DAY: It was overwritten. Paved over. But the data's still here. The corporation didn't delete the past — they just stacked the present on top of it.

NEURAL BURN: Look at the timestamps. The old city's clock is still running. It's been counting for years, alone, in the dark.

ZERO-DAY: Counting toward what?

A transmission flickers into focus — a familiar cadence. The seal. The voice. Zero-Day goes cold.

ZERO-DAY: Neural. Pull that up. The last one.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The old city's skyline rendered in dead data, one transmission glowing at its center.]

---

[SCENE 4 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

The recording plays on every screen in the basement. The four stand in a loose circle, frozen. It's the emergency broadcast — the *credit conversion* — but it's wrong. The seal is older. The cadence is the same. Word for word.

BROADCAST VOICE: (crackling, older) Citizens of the municipal district. The one-world currency is complete. As of this broadcast, all physical cash is void...

SWARM TECH: That's the conversion. That's *our* conversion.

ZERO-DAY: It's dated years earlier.

GHOST WALKER: Years before the flood.

NEURAL BURN: It's happened before. The conversion. The credits. The grid. All of it.

SWARM TECH: No. That's a coincidence. A dry run. A—a rehearsal.

ZERO-DAY: It's word-for-word identical, Swarm. Every phrase. Every pause. This isn't a rehearsal. This is the *same event*, broadcast before the one we lived through.

GHOST WALKER: They've run the conversion before.

NEURAL BURN: They've run *everything* before.

The four exchange a look — the same look from the ledger, from the wiped node, from the photograph. The pieces are locking together, and none of them want to finish the sentence.

ZERO-DAY: The city didn't just have a past. It's had *this* past. Ours. Before us.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The four faces lit by the old broadcast, the older seal glowing on the screens.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

Neural Burn isolates the recording, cleaning the static until it's crystal clear. The final line of the old broadcast rings out in the basement, and there's no mistaking it.

BROADCAST VOICE: (clear, final) ...for public safety and transactional integrity.

Silence. Then Zero-Day speaks, very quietly.

ZERO-DAY: The conversion has happened before. The surveillance. The credits. Maybe even the war. All of it.

SWARM TECH: If it's happened before, then what are we?

GHOST WALKER: The latest run.

NEURAL BURN: The static's been trying to tell me since the beginning. The voice. The name. The recordings. It's not prophecy. It's *memory*. The whole city is one machine that remembers everything — including the last time we lost.

ZERO-DAY: And Kairos—

NEURAL BURN: Kairos doesn't predict us, Zero-Day. He's *replaying* us.

Zero-Day's head blips — the hologram skull flickering, longer and longer each time. He stares at the old broadcast's timestamp, and it stares back.

ZERO-DAY: Then we've already lost. We just don't remember the fight.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The timestamp of the old broadcast, Zero-Day's flickering hologram head beside it.]

---

[CLIFFHANGER: One recording resolves into crystal clarity — a previous emergency broadcast, word-for-word identical to the credit conversion, but dated years earlier; the conversion has happened before.]
