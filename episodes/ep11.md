[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

The reset's first wave has left the crew reeling. Outside, the city is forgetting itself in small, terrible ways. Inside, Zero-Day is already reaching for the Key-Staff, and there's a finality in the motion.

ZERO-DAY: I'm going down. To the deepest layer of the Root. To kill the reset at its source.

SWARM TECH: The source? That's the corporation's central brain. You're walking into the heart of it alone.

ZERO-DAY: I'm the only one who can. The Key-Staff opens the Root. And whatever's at the bottom — whatever's running the loop — I have to face it.

GHOST WALKER: I'll go with you.

ZERO-DAY: No. The deeper layers aren't for bodies. They're for... whatever I am. If I'm the afterimage of the last Zero-Day, then I'm the only thing that belongs down there.

NEURAL BURN: I can guide you. I can hear the layers. I'll be your eyes when yours give out.

ZERO-DAY: Then stay on the line with me. But no one follows. This is the end of the thread, and I have to pull it alone.

Swarm Tech opens his mouth to argue, then closes it. The room is quiet. The staff hums.

ZERO-DAY: If I don't come back — don't reset me. Let me be the last version.

[SCENE 2 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

Swarm Tech steps forward, and for once there's no bravado in him — just the raw, tired love of a man who's run out of ways to keep his friend.

SWARM TECH: I've followed you into everything, Zero. The hub. The terminal. The deep layers. I'm not going to just stand here and watch you walk into the dark alone.

ZERO-DAY: You have to. This part's mine. It's always been mine — that's the whole point of the loop, Swarm. It ends where I am. I'm the one thing it was built around.

GHOST WALKER: Then let us at least hold the surface. Keep the corporation off you while you go down.

ZERO-DAY: Do that. Hold the Seawall. Hold the entry. And Neural —

NEURAL BURN: I'll be on the line. Every layer. I won't let go of your signal, Zero. Not until you tell me to.

ZERO-DAY: (quiet) If you hear me go quiet... don't come looking. Whatever's down there, it's not something any of you can pull me out of. It's something I have to let go of myself.

He raises the Key-Staff, and it answers — a low, rising hum that fills the basement, and the green water on the floor ripples outward from his feet as if the city itself is leaning in to listen.

ZERO-DAY: Eleven seconds. I opened every door once. Now I'm going to close the one that's been open the whole time.

[GENERATE: Same basement, continuous. Zero-Day raises the Key-Staff and it answers with a low rising hum, the green water rippling outward from his feet, the crew faces lit with fearful hope. Wide shot as the water ripples. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day raising the Key-Staff, the water rippling at his feet, the crew's faces lit with a hope they're afraid to feel.]

[SCENE 3 — INT. ROOT DEEP LAYERS — VIA THE STAFF — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → the Root's deepest layers]

The descent is vertigo. Zero-Day plunges through the Root's layers — past the living grid, past the old broadcast layer, past the preserved city — into strata so deep the code isn't code anymore. It's geology. It's pressure. Neural Burn's voice threads alongside him, thin but steady.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) You're through the memory layer. Below it there's nothing on any map. I can barely hear you.

ZERO-DAY: I can feel it. It's cold down here.

NEURAL BURN: The cold is the point. The core's meant to be empty. Don't trust what it shows you.

ZERO-DAY: What is it supposed to show me?

NEURAL BURN: (a pause) Whatever you brought with you.

Zero-Day descends, and the layers fold past him like the pages of a drowned book — the grid, the ledgers, the broadcasts, the old city, and beneath all of it, a darkness that has no name.

[GENERATE: Root deep layers at night, a vertiginous plunge through the grid, the old broadcast layer, and the preserved city, code turning to geology. Zero-Day descends, the layers folding past like a drowned book. Wide shot of Zero-Day plunging. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day plunging through the layers, code turning to geology around him, Neural Burn's voice a thin thread in the dark.]

[SCENE 4 — INT. ROOT DEEP LAYERS — CONTINUOUS]

The pressure builds, and the layers thin, and in the deep a faint shape resolves — not a machine, but a *figure*. A DATA ECHO, half-formed, the afterimage of someone who passed this way long ago, preserved in the Root's memory like a fossil in stone.

DATA ECHO: (faint, ancient) ...you're early. The door's not supposed to open for another cycle...

ZERO-DAY: Who are you?

DATA ECHO: I was the one before the one before you. Or after. It's hard to tell, down here. The Root keeps us all, and we all blur together in the cold.

ZERO-DAY: What's at the bottom?

DATA ECHO: Nothing. That's the thing they never told you. The whole city, the whole war, and at the center of it — nothing. Just a socket. Just a space shaped like the thing that's supposed to fill it.

ZERO-DAY: Shaped like what?

DATA ECHO: (fading) Shaped like you, hacker. It's always been shaped like you. Now hurry. Before the cold takes your name the way it took mine.

The echo dissolves into the dark, and Zero-Day is alone again, and the cold is immense.

[GENERATE: Same deep layers, continuous. A half-formed data echo resolves in the cold, its face a blur of old code, dissolving into the dark. Close on the fading echo, then Zero-Day passing through. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The fading data echo, its half-formed face a blur of old code, Zero-Day passing through it into the dark.]

[SCENE 5 — INT. ROOT DEEP LAYERS — CONTINUOUS]

Zero-Day presses on, and the pressure becomes unbearable, and then — with a sound like a held breath released — the last layer tears open. He is through.

He lands in a vast dark space, featureless, silent. The heart of the city. The center of everything.

It is empty.

ZERO-DAY: (comms) Neural. I'm through. There's nothing here.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) Nothing?

ZERO-DAY: It's a room. A huge, black, empty room. No servers. No machines. Just... space.

He takes a step, and his footstep doesn't echo. The floor is black and polished, and it swallows sound the way the loop swallows memory. Overhead, a faint ring of light hangs in the dark, the only feature in the whole vast nothing.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) That can't be. The whole city runs on the Root. It has to have a brain.

ZERO-DAY: I'll keep looking. There has to be something.

[GENERATE: Same deep layers, continuous. The last layer tears open and Zero-Day lands in a vast black space, featureless and silent, his footstep not echoing, a faint ring of light overhead. Wide shot of him alone in the emptiness. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day alone in the vast black core, the faint ring of light overhead, his reflection a small shadow on the polished floor.]

[SCENE 6 — INT. THE CORE — NIGHT]

Zero-Day walks into the dark, and his footsteps don't echo. There is no server. No master computer. No god of machines. There is only a flat black floor, a faint ring of light overhead, and — at the very center — a socket. A simple, waiting socket, shaped for a single key.

ZERO-DAY: (comms) Neural. There's nothing here.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) That can't be. The whole city runs on the Root. It has to have a brain.

ZERO-DAY: It doesn't. There's no machine. There's a socket. And me.

He steps to the socket and looks at the Key-Staff in his fist. The socket's shape matches the staff exactly. And as he holds the staff over it, the socket begins to glow — not from below, not from a power source. From *him*.

ZERO-DAY: It's not reading the staff. It's reading me.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) Zero... what are you saying?

ZERO-DAY: I thought I'd find the engine that runs the loop. But the engine isn't a machine down here. The engine is—

He stops. His own reflection in the polished black floor flickers into a hologram skull, and this time it holds. Fully. Steadily. As if the core were turning up a light on what he's always been.

[GENERATE: The core at night, a flat black floor swallowing sound, a faint ring of light overhead, and at the center a socket shaped for a single key. Zero-Day steps toward it, the Key-Staff matching. Close on the socket as it begins to glow. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day at the socket, the staff matching it, the glow rising from his own chest.]

[SCENE 7 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

Kairos's voice arrives — not through comms, but through the core itself, as if the room were speaking.

KAIROS: (disembodied) You wanted to see the source. Here it is. There is no central brain, Zero-Day. There never was.

ZERO-DAY: Then what runs the loop?

KAIROS: You do. The socket matches the Key-Staff. The Key-Staff is not a key at all. It is a *tether* — a length of the Root, made to bind an afterimage to the machine. And you have been carrying the engine of the reset in your own fist this whole time.

ZERO-DAY: No. I fight the reset. I'm trying to *kill* it.

KAIROS: You are the reset. The loop closes because you exist. The core lights because you're standing in it. You are not the hacker who broke the Root. You are the mechanism that lets it be broken — and rebuilt — forever.

Zero-Day stares at the staff, then at his own glowing hands. The truth is a cold weight settling in his chest.

ZERO-DAY: I'm the reason it can reset at all.

KAIROS: Now you understand the clock. It doesn't tick forward. It ticks around you.

[GENERATE: Same core, continuous. Kairos disembodied voice fills the dark as Zero-Day realizes the staff is a tether, his own glowing hands reflected in the black floor. Close on Zero-Day reflection. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day's glowing form reflected in the black floor, the socket blazing, Kairos's voice filling the dark.]

---

[SCENE 8 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

Zero-Day's head blazes into full light — the hologram skull steady, no longer a glitch, no longer a flicker. He holds the staff over the socket, and the whole dark space begins to thrum with the thing that has been powering the city's endless war.

ZERO-DAY: If I'm the engine, then the previous holders — the man in the photograph, the man in the footage — they were engines too. Every one of them.

KAIROS: Every one. The Key-Staff passes from afterimage to afterimage, and the loop passes with it. The corporation doesn't create the Zero-Day. It *receives* him, each cycle, from the Root's memory. And it tends him, the way one tends a flame that must not go out.

ZERO-DAY: Because if the flame goes out—

KAIROS: The loop ends. And what the loop contains — what it has contained for a generation of generations — is released.

ZERO-DAY: (quiet) You keep saying that. "Contained." What's out there, Kairos? What is the loop keeping in?

KAIROS: (a long silence) Something the city was never meant to meet. But that is a question for after. First — you must understand what you are, fully. Because you cannot choose until you know.

The socket blazes brighter, and the ring of light overhead begins to turn, slow, in a circle. The core is waking, and it is waking *to* Zero-Day — to the afterimage at its center, the engine in the empty room.

[GENERATE: Same core, continuous. Zero-Day head blazes full, the hologram skull steady, as the ring of light overhead begins to turn and the socket drinks the light from his chest. Wide shot of Zero-Day blazing at the center. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day blazing at the center, the ring of light turning, the socket drinking the light from his chest.]

[SCENE 9 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

Up above, the crew hears only Zero-Day's half of it, and it is enough to hollow them out. Neural Burn relays what he can, his own voice breaking.

NEURAL BURN: He's at the core. There's nothing there. No machine. Just a socket and the staff. And it's... it's him, Swarm. The loop runs through him.

SWARM TECH: That's not possible. He's one of us. He's our friend. He's the one who *started* this.

NEURAL BURN: He didn't start it. He's the part that repeats it.

GHOST WALKER: Then we have to get him out of there. If he's the engine, breaking the staff—

SWARM TECH: Breaking the staff could kill him. Or kill all of us. We don't know what happens when the engine stops.

NEURAL BURN: He knows. And he's still down there, looking at the socket, deciding.

The three fall silent, listening to the dark, waiting for the word from the bottom of the world.

[GENERATE: Basement, later that night, the three NU11 gathered around the comms, listening, hollowed out, the basement lit only by the faint glow of the Root. Close on Neural Burn breaking face. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[SCENE 10 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

Swarm Tech can't stay still. He paces, and the silence is worse than any firefight.

SWARM TECH: This is what he meant. "Let me be the last version." He knew. He knew going down there that the engine was him.

NEURAL BURN: He suspected. Now he's sure.

GHOST WALKER: And he's still choosing. That's the part that matters. The loop says every version of him resets. He's down there right now, holding the tether, and he hasn't reset yet.

SWARM TECH: Yet. There's a "yet" on the end of that.

NEURAL BURN: There's always been a "yet." That's the whole shape of the thing. The loop doesn't end because it's unbreakable. It ends because every Zero-Day, at the last second, chooses to keep it. We've been watching him choose wrong for a hundred cycles, and we never knew.

SWARM TECH: (fierce) Then maybe this time we don't just watch. Maybe this time we tell him — from up here, all of us — that he doesn't have to choose wrong.

GHOST WALKER: He can hear you. Neural's holding the line.

Swarm Tech grabs the comm, and his voice shakes.

SWARM TECH: Zero. It's Swarm. I know you can hear me. Don't you dare reset. Don't you dare choose wrong again. We're the good guys. Remember? You told me that. So be the good guy. *Choose different.*

[GENERATE: Same basement, continuous. Swarm Tech paces, then grabs the comm, his voice shaking as he pleads into the dark. Close on Swarm Tech gripping the comm. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Swarm Tech gripping the comm, the three of them crowded around it, their voices reaching down into the dark.]

[SCENE 11 — INT. THE CORE — NIGHT]

Zero-Day stands at the socket, the staff half-raised, the light pouring from his own chest into the black. The hologram head burns steady now — no glitch, no flicker. Just the truth, lit up. And faintly, from above, Swarm Tech's voice threads through the dark, and he hears it.

ZERO-DAY: If I'm the engine, then the only way to stop the loop... is to stop me.

KAIROS: (disembodied) Now you see the choice every version of you has faced. And every version has failed it. They all chose to reset. They all chose to keep the city, and themselves, running.

ZERO-DAY: Why? Why would they choose that?

KAIROS: Because the loop is not just a prison, Zero-Day. It is a *containment*. What is outside it — beyond the seawall, past the edge of the map — is worse. Every previous you looked at that socket and chose to keep the wall standing.

ZERO-DAY: Then maybe this time I choose different.

KAIROS: (softly) Maybe. No version has, yet.

[GENERATE: The core, night, Zero-Day at the socket, staff half-raised, light pouring from his own chest, Swarm Tech voice a faint thread reaching him. Close on Zero-Day listening in the dark. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day at the socket, the staff half-raised, Swarm Tech's voice a faint thread reaching him through the dark.]

[SCENE 12 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

The core lights up fully — not from a server, but from Zero-Day himself. The socket blazes. The staff sings. And the whole dark space begins to hum with the thing that has been powering the city's endless war all along.

ZERO-DAY: It's me. It's been me the whole time.

KAIROS: Since before the flood. Since before the first cycle that anyone remembers. You are the afterimage, the engine, and the key. And you are standing, for the hundredth time, at the only door that matters.

ZERO-DAY: What's on the other side of it?

KAIROS: You'll have to decide whether to find out. The staff in your hand can reset the city — as it always has — or it can break, and release whatever the wall has been holding back. There is no third option. That is the cruelty of the loop. It has always offered you two choices, and you have always, always, chosen the cage.

Zero-Day looks at his own hands — glowing, shaking, no longer entirely his own — and then at the socket, and then up, toward the thread of Swarm Tech's voice still reaching down through the black.

ZERO-DAY: Then it's time I chose something new.

The socket pulses. The staff hums. And the ring of light overhead stops turning — and holds — waiting.

[GENERATE: Same core, continuous. The core lights fully from Zero-Day himself, the socket blazing, the ring of light overhead stopping and holding. Wide shot of Zero-Day blazing at the center. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day blazing at the center of the core, the socket and staff fused in light, the black around him lit to the edges.]

[SCENE 13 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

Zero-Day looks up through the dark, toward the thread of his crew's voices, and asks the question he's been circling all night.

ZERO-DAY: Kairos. If I break the staff — if the loop stops — what happens to the city? To them?

KAIROS: (disembodied) The city survives. The city has always survived. What changes is what it survives *as*. A cage, or a free thing. That is the choice, Zero-Day. Not whether the city lives. Whether it lives *free*.

ZERO-DAY: And the cost?

KAIROS: The cost is the wall. Whatever the loop was containing — it will come. But you knew that. You've always known, in the part of you that remembers every cycle.

ZERO-DAY: Then I choose. Not for the city. For *them*. For the ones who never got to choose.

[GENERATE: Same core, continuous. Zero-Day looks up through the dark toward his crew voices, the choice settling into him like a weight. Close on Zero-Day face as the choice lands. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day in the core's dark, the socket blazing, the choice settling into him like a weight.]

[SCENE 14 — INT. BASEMENT — CONTINUOUS]

Up above, the crew can hear the weight of it in the silence on the line. Swarm Tech's plea hangs unanswered. Neural Burn's cables spark, and his burned mind is full of the coming thing.

NEURAL BURN: He's decided. I can feel it. The loop's never felt this before — a version of him that isn't running the same script.

SWARM TECH: Is he going to break it?

NEURAL BURN: I don't know. But whatever he does — it won't be what the loop wrote. That's the only thing that matters.

GHOST WALKER: Then we hold. For as long as he needs.

SWARM TECH: (quiet) And if he doesn't come back?

NEURAL BURN: Then we make sure it meant something. We remember. For all of us. Even when the city can't.

[GENERATE: Basement, continuous, the three NU11 listening to the silence on the line, Neural Burn cables sparking, the comm humming faint with the core light. Wide shot of the three waiting. Arcane-inspired 3D animated, dark cyberpunk.]

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The three in the basement, listening to the dark, the comm line humming faint with the core's light.]

[SCENE 15 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

Zero-Day closes his eyes, and hears them — Swarm Tech's ragged hope, Ghost Walker's silence, Neural Burn's steady, burned clarity. And he hears, beneath it all, the loop's old, patient hum, the song it has sung through a hundred cycles of the same war.

ZERO-DAY: (soft, to the dark) This is the end of the thread. I can feel it. Whatever I am — afterimage, engine, man — I'm standing at the last door. And I'm not going to walk through it the way they all did.

He opens his eyes. The socket blazes. The staff hums in his grip, and for the first time, the hum feels less like a tether and more like a question — waiting, at last, for an answer that isn't written down.

ZERO-DAY: It's time to find out what happens when the loop meets a version of me that refuses to reset.
