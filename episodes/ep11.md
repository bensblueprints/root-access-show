# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 11 — "empty_core"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

The reset's first wave has left the crew reeling. Outside, the city is forgetting itself in small, terrible ways. Inside, Zero-Day is already reaching for the Key-Staff, and there's a finality in the motion.

ZERO-DAY: I'm going down. To the deepest layer of the Root. To kill the reset at its source.

SWARM TECH: The source? That's the corporation's central brain. You're walking into the heart of it alone.

ZERO-DAY: I'm the only one who can. The Key-Staff opens the Root. And whatever's at the bottom — whatever's running the loop — I have to face it.

GHOST WALKER: I'll go with you.

ZERO-DAY: No. The deeper layers aren't for bodies. They're for... whatever I am. If I'm the afterimage of the last Zero-Day, then I'm the only thing that belongs down there.

NEURAL BURN: I can guide you. I can hear the layers. I'll be your eyes when yours give out.

ZERO-DAY: Then stay on the line with me. But no one follows. This is the end of the thread, and I have to pull it alone.

Swarm Tech opens his mouth to argue, then closes it. The room is quiet. The staff hums.

ZERO-DAY: If I don't come back — don't reset me. Let me be the last version.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day gripping the Key-Staff, the crew gathered but held back, the staff already beginning to glow.]

---

[SCENE 2 — INT. ROOT DEEP LAYERS — VIA THE STAFF — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → the Root's deepest layers]

The descent is vertigo. Zero-Day plunges through the Root's layers — past the living grid, past the old broadcast layer, past the preserved city — into strata so deep the code isn't code anymore. It's geology. It's pressure. Neural Burn's voice threads alongside him, thin but steady.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) You're through the memory layer. Below it there's nothing on any map. I can barely hear you.

ZERO-DAY: I can feel it. It's cold down here.

NEURAL BURN: The cold is the point. The core's meant to be empty. Don't trust what it shows you.

ZERO-DAY: What is it supposed to show me?

NEURAL BURN: (a pause) Whatever you brought with you.

The pressure builds. The last layer tears open — and Zero-Day is through. He lands in a vast dark space, featureless, silent. The heart of the city. The center of everything.

It is empty.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day at the threshold of the core, a vast black nothing stretching before him.]

---

[SCENE 3 — INT. THE CORE — NIGHT]

Zero-Day walks into the dark, and his footsteps don't echo. There is no server. No master computer. No god of machines. There is only a flat black floor, a faint ring of light overhead, and — at the very center — a socket. A simple, waiting socket, shaped for a single key.

ZERO-DAY: (comms) Neural. There's nothing here.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) That can't be. The whole city runs on the Root. It has to have a brain.

ZERO-DAY: It doesn't. There's no machine. There's a socket. And me.

He steps to the socket and looks at the Key-Staff in his fist. The socket's shape matches the staff exactly. And as he holds the staff over it, the socket begins to glow — not from below, not from a power source. From *him*.

ZERO-DAY: It's not reading the staff. It's reading me.

NEURAL BURN: (comms) Zero... what are you saying?

ZERO-DAY: I thought I'd find the engine that runs the loop. But the engine isn't a machine down here. The engine is—

He stops. His own reflection in the polished black floor flickers into a hologram skull, and this time it holds. Fully. Steadily. As if the core were turning up a light on what he's always been.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day at the socket, the staff matching it, the glow rising from his own chest.]

---

[SCENE 4 — INT. THE CORE — CONTINUOUS]

Kairos's voice arrives — not through comms, but through the core itself, as if the room were speaking.

KAIROS: (disembodied) You wanted to see the source. Here it is. There is no central brain, Zero-Day. There never was.

ZERO-DAY: Then what runs the loop?

KAIROS: You do. The socket matches the Key-Staff. The Key-Staff is not a key at all. It is a *tether* — a length of the Root, made to bind an afterimage to the machine. And you have been carrying the engine of the reset in your own fist this whole time.

ZERO-DAY: No. I fight the reset. I'm trying to *kill* it.

KAIROS: You are the reset. The loop closes because you exist. The core lights because you're standing in it. You are not the hacker who broke the Root. You are the mechanism that lets it be broken — and rebuilt — forever.

Zero-Day stares at the staff, then at his own glowing hands. The truth is a cold weight settling in his chest.

ZERO-DAY: I'm the reason it can reset at all.

KAIROS: Now you understand the clock. It doesn't tick forward. It ticks around you.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day's glowing form reflected in the black floor, the socket blazing, Kairos's voice filling the dark.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

[TIME: later that night]

Up above, the crew hears only Zero-Day's half of it, and it is enough to hollow them out. Neural Burn relays what he can, his own voice breaking.

NEURAL BURN: He's at the core. There's nothing there. No machine. Just a socket and the staff. And it's... it's him, Swarm. The loop runs through him.

SWARM TECH: That's not possible. He's one of us. He's our friend. He's the one who *started* this.

NEURAL BURN: He didn't start it. He's the part that repeats it.

GHOST WALKER: Then we have to get him out of there. If he's the engine, breaking the staff—

SWARM TECH: Breaking the staff could kill him. Or kill all of us. We don't know what happens when the engine stops.

NEURAL BURN: He knows. And he's still down there, looking at the socket, deciding.

The three fall silent, listening to the dark, waiting for the word from the bottom of the world.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The three NU11 gathered around the comms, listening, the basement lit only by the faint glow of the Root.]

---

[SCENE 6 — INT. THE CORE — NIGHT]

Zero-Day stands at the socket, the staff half-raised, the light pouring from his own chest into the black. The hologram head burns steady now — no glitch, no flicker. Just the truth, lit up.

ZERO-DAY: If I'm the engine, then the only way to stop the loop... is to stop me.

KAIROS: (disembodied) Now you see the choice every version of you has faced. And every version has failed it. They all chose to reset. They all chose to keep the city, and themselves, running.

ZERO-DAY: Why? Why would they choose that?

KAIROS: Because the loop is not just a prison, Zero-Day. It is a *containment*. What is outside it — beyond the seawall, past the edge of the map — is worse. Every previous you looked at that socket and chose to keep the wall standing.

ZERO-DAY: Then maybe this time I choose different.

KAIROS: (softly) Maybe. No version has, yet.

The core lights up fully — not from a server, but from Zero-Day himself. The socket blazes. The staff sings. And the whole dark space begins to hum with the thing that has been powering the city's endless war all along.

ZERO-DAY: It's me. It's been me the whole time.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Zero-Day blazing at the center of the core, the socket and staff fused in light, the black around him lit to the edges.]
