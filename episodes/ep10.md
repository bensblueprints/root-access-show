# ROOT ACCESS — EPISODE 10 — "reset"

---

[SCENE 1 — INT. BASEMENT — NIGHT]

Zero-Day is back, and he has emptied the whole awful truth onto the table: the loop, the afterimage, the reset already scheduled. The four are moving before the shock can settle.

ZERO-DAY: Kairos is going to trigger another reset. A second conversion. He'll wipe the city's memory and run the war again — and us with it.

SWARM TECH: How long do we have?

ZERO-DAY: He said "the cycle." He didn't give a clock. But the signs are already up — the grid's re-indexing, the ledger's re-issuing. It's starting.

GHOST WALKER: Then we stop it at the source. The Seawall is where the old city's signal bleeds through. That's the reset's trigger path.

SWARM TECH: I'll take the swarm to the Seawall. Keep their drones off Zero-Day's back while he works.

ZERO-DAY: Neural, you're the only one who can *see* the reset coming. If the city starts to forget itself, you'll be the only one who remembers.

NEURAL BURN: I've been remembering for all of us since the beginning.

GHOST WALKER: And the neighborhood sweeps — they're clearing the Sprawl block by block, looking for us. Someone has to keep them off the civilians.

ZERO-DAY: Then go. All of you. This is the last fight. One way or another.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The four pulling on their gear, the basement already feeling like a place they're about to lose.]

---

[SCENE 2 — EXT. THE SEAWALL — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: basement → the Seawall]

The breach where the sea came in. Wind, salt, the roar of black water against the concrete. Swarm Tech stands on the wall, hand raised, and his swarm rises around him in a storm of rotors. Across the water, NEXUS COMMANDER steps out with his own Overwatch — spotter drones whispering at his shoulder, command baton raised.

NEXUS COMMANDER: You. The one who thinks machines are music. Stand down, and I'll let the flood keep your body.

SWARM TECH: You ever conduct anything, Commander? Or do you just point and hope?

NEXUS COMMANDER: I point, and order prevails. That's the difference between us.

SWARM TECH: The difference between us is I know what I'm fighting for.

He brings his hand down, and the two swarms collide over the black water — rotors screaming, drones tearing each other apart in a spiral of sparks and salt spray. It's beautiful and terrible at once.

NEXUS COMMANDER: (into comms) All units — I want the drone-controller's position. I want him in pieces.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Swarm Tech on the Seawall conducting, his swarm and the Overwatch locked in a spiral over the water.]

---

[SCENE 3 — EXT. THE SPRAWL — BLOCK CLEARANCE — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: the Seawall → a Sprawl neighborhood]

A residential block. Families, stalls, water running through the streets. ARC SENTINEL stands at the head of a white-plated line, the clearance order crackling in his helmet. The polite word for what's about to happen is "deterrence."

ARC SENTINEL: (over loudspeaker) By order of Municipal Security, this block is scheduled for clearance. Evacuate immediately.

Among the line is the NEXUS TROOPER — young, poor, from this very neighborhood not long ago. His faceplate is up, and he's looking at a woman in a doorway who could be his own mother.

NEXUS TROOPER: Sentinel... these are civilians. There's no confirmed rebel presence here.

ARC SENTINEL: The order says clear it.

NEXUS TROOPER: The order's wrong. My cousin lives three doors down. I know every face on this street.

ARC SENTINEL: (quieter) So did I, once. Put your faceplate down and do the job.

NEXUS TROOPER: The job is clearing people's homes. That's not a job. That's—

ARC SENTINEL: That's the *line*, Trooper. And we don't get to redraw it because it hurts.

The trooper's hands shake on his pulse rifle. He doesn't lower the faceplate.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: The trooper's raised faceplate, the faces of the neighborhood reflected in it, Arc Sentinel's silhouette beside him.]

---

[SCENE 4 — EXT. THE SPRAWL — ROOFTOP ABOVE THE BLOCK — NIGHT]

[ROOM CHANGE: street → rooftop]

Ghost Walker moves above the clearance, glitch-camo swallowing the rooftop shadows, rail-pistol ready. Below, the sweep is turning ugly — a resident pushes back, a rifle comes up, and the line tenses. In the chaos, the Nexus Trooper raises his own weapon — not at a rebel, but to stop one of his own from firing on a child.

Ghost Walker sees only the white plating, the raised rifle, the angle. One shot to save the block. One clean kill, the way he's always done it.

GHOST WALKER: (to himself, steadying) I only remove the guilty.

He fires. The neural spike streaks down. The Nexus Trooper's faceplate — still up — turns toward the sound, and for one frame the two of them lock eyes: the assassin and the line soldier, the same poor kid from the lower city, a mirror across the barrel.

The trooper drops. The block goes silent. And Ghost Walker, for the first time, stops counting.

GHOST WALKER: (a whisper, breaking) That wasn't the guilty one.

Arc Sentinel kneels over the fallen trooper, and something in the true believer finally cracks open.

ARC SENTINEL: He was just doing the job. He was just—

The order to clear the neighborhood is still running. The reset is still coming. And below, a mother is pulling her child out of the water, and the child is crying, and nobody's counting anymore except the machine.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Ghost Walker frozen on the rooftop, the fallen trooper below, Arc Sentinel kneeling beside him.]

---

[SCENE 5 — INT. ARCOLOGY — KAIROS'S COMMAND — NIGHT]

[LOCATION CHANGE: the Sprawl → the Arcology]

Kairos stands at the wall of screens. The reset sequence is live — a countdown rendered as a slow, circular sweep, not a falling number. Nexus Commander's voice crackles in.

NEXUS COMMANDER: (comms) Director, the drone-controller is holding the Seawall. The assassin has gone dark. Do I pursue?

KAIROS: No. Let them run. The reset does not need them dead, Commander. It needs them *present*. A rebellion with no rebels is a failed harvest.

NEXUS COMMANDER: Then what are my orders?

KAIROS: Hold the line until the first wave. Then watch the city forget.

He reaches out and touches the screen — the countdown circle, the sweep, the clock that only ever ticks in a circle.

KAIROS: Begin.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Kairos's hand on the circular countdown, the wall of screens beginning to shift.]

---

[SCENE 6 — EXT. THE SPRAWL — STREET — NIGHT]

[TIME: the reset's first wave]

The first wave hits — and it is almost invisible. Not a blast. A *flicker*. A street sign blurs and re-forms in an older font. A shopfront's name changes to one that hasn't existed in years. The skyline stutters, a building's reflection holding a shape from before the flood. Citizens sway, blinking, as if a dream had passed over them.

No one notices. They keep walking, keep arguing, keep surviving — their memories already beginning to blur at the edges.

Except one.

Neural Burn stands in the middle of the flickering street, his jacks sparking, his burned brain lit up like a struck match. He sees all of it.

NEURAL BURN: (loud, to the crowd) It's happening! The city is forgetting! Look at the signs — look at the skyline — it's not yours, it's the *old* one bleeding through!

A passerby glances at him, then away. The world keeps its own rhythm.

NEURAL BURN: (softer, realizing) You can't see it. None of you can. It's wiping you, and you don't even feel it.

He turns in a slow circle as the street around him rewrites itself. He is the only mind in the city the loop can't fully overwrite — because his was already burned, already scarred, already too broken to erase.

NEURAL BURN: I remember. I remember all of it. And I'm not going to let it take me this time.

[REFERENCE IMAGE: Neural Burn alone in the flickering street, the city rewriting itself around him while he alone watches.]
